What you get

Built for timing first

Most video calls smear music. air.band sends your instrument raw and measures every millisecond, so you can hear where the delay goes and fix it.

Real time

Under 30 ms between nearby players: it feels like the same room. Straight from browser to browser, or app to app.

BARS

Trade 4, 8 or 16 bars. Whoever's soloing is always on the beat for everyone, whatever the distance.

An AI band

Pick a track or describe one. Take a part and the AI steps out; transpose or change the tempo any time.

Record it

The whole jam, with video, plus a separate track per player for your DAW.
